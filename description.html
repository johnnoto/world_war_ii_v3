A new edition of World War II with new tech and units. Includes three starting scenarios: 1939, 1941 and 1942.

The 1939 scenario starts on 1 September 1939 with France as a playable power, invadable neutral countries, and historical diplomacy: Italy, the Soviet Union, Japan and the United States enter the war through declarations of war in the Politics phase, and events such as the Fall of France and Vichy, the Liberation of Paris, Romania and Bulgaria joining the Axis, and the Dutch and Belgian governments in exile fire automatically.
