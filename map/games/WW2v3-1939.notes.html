World War II v3 – September 1939

1 September 1939. The Wehrmacht crosses into Poland. Two days later Britain and France declare war. The Soviet Union has just signed a non-aggression pact with Germany, Italy is a "non-belligerent" bound to Berlin by the Pact of Steel, Japan has been at war in China for two years, and the United States is officially neutral. Nobody yet knows how wide this war will become – that is up to you.

France is a full playable power in this scenario, with its own army, fleet and colonial empire. One round is roughly six months: round 1 is autumn 1939, round 2 spring–summer 1940, round 4 summer 1941, round 5 the winter of 1941–42.

Neutral Countries

Poland, East Poland, the Low Countries, Norway, Finland, the Baltic States, Yugoslavia and Greece (Balkans), Persia and Iceland begin neutral. Most have armies of their own (orange units) and can be invaded by anyone at no PU cost – they are at war with whoever attacks them. Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey and the other impassable neutrals stay out of the war.

Romania and Bulgaria, the Dutch East Indies (Borneo, East Indies) and the Belgian Congo are sealed until history opens them (see Events).

Diplomacy

Powers that are at peace with each other cannot attack each other or enter each other's territory. War is declared in the Politics phase, and only when history allows it:

  • Italy enters the war (round 2+, June 1940) – against Britain, and against France if France is still fighting.
  • Operation Barbarossa – Germany may attack the USSR from round 4 (June 1941).
  • Soviet pre-emptive war – the USSR may declare war on Germany from round 6 if Germany has not attacked.
  • Occupation of Indochina – Japan may go to war with France from round 3 (September 1940).
  • Strike North – Japan may attack the USSR from round 3.
  • Strike South – Japan may attack Britain and the United States together from round 5 (December 1941).
  • Germany declares war on the USA from round 5.
  • The United States enters the war against Germany from round 6, or at once if the Axis take the United Kingdom; against Japan from round 6, or at once if Japan attacks Britain; against Italy once at war with Germany.
  • August Storm – the USSR may attack Japan from round 11 (August 1945), or once Germany has fallen.
  • Operation Catapult – Britain may attack Vichy France. Operation Torch – the United States, once at war, may land in Vichy territory. Case Anton – Germany may occupy Vichy France.

Alliances form automatically between powers fighting a common enemy: Germany and Italy once Italy enters the war, Britain and the USSR after Barbarossa, the Grand Alliance after Pearl Harbor, and so on. Vichy France joins no one.

Events

  • Fall of France. If the Axis hold France at the start of the French turn while France is still fighting, the Armistice of Compiègne is signed: France becomes the neutral Vichy regime and stops collecting income.
  • Liberation of Paris. If Britain, the USA or the USSR later take France from the Axis, the French government returns to the war on the Allied side and France is handed back to it.
  • Governments in exile. When the Axis take the Low Countries, the Dutch East Indies and the Belgian Congo join Britain, each Dutch colony with a garrison.
  • Romania and Bulgaria join the Axis at the start of Germany's round 3 combat move, with their armies (3 infantry, 1 artillery).
  • Italy automatically declares war on the USSR when Germany is at war with it. Germany and Italy declare war on the United States once Japan is at war with Britain and the USA.

National Objectives

Germany

  • +5 Soviet Trade Agreement: while not at war with the USSR.
  • +5 Four-Year Plan: Germany controls Germany and Czechoslovakia-Hungary.
  • +5 Fortress Europe: Axis control France, NW Europe, Germany, Poland, Czechoslovakia-Hungary and Bulgaria-Romania.
  • +5 Lebensraum: Axis control 3 of Baltic States, East Poland, Belorussia, Eastern Ukraine, Ukraine.
  • +5 Axis control Karelia or the Caucasus.

Soviet Union (only while at war with Germany)

  • +5 Great Patriotic War: Soviets control Archangel and no Allied units are in Soviet territory.
  • +10 Allies control 3 of Norway, Finland, Poland, Bulgaria-Romania, Czechoslovakia-Hungary, Balkans.

Japan

  • +5 Axis control Manchuria, Kiangsu and French Indo-China.
  • +5 Axis control 4 of Kwangtung, East Indies, Borneo, Philippines, New Guinea, Solomon Islands.
  • +5 Axis control 1 of Hawaii, Australia, India.

Britain

  • +5 Allies control any of Caroline Islands, Formosa, Iwo Jima, Japan, Okinawa.
  • +5 Allies control Eastern and Western Canada, Gibraltar, Egypt, Australia and South Africa.
  • +5 Allies control France or the Balkans.

France

  • +3 French control Morocco-Algeria, French West Africa, French Equatorial Africa, Madagascar and Indo-China.

Italy

  • +5 Axis control 3 of Egypt, Trans-Jordan, France, Gibraltar.
  • +5 Axis control Italy, Balkans, Morocco-Algeria and Libya, with no enemy surface warships in sea zones 13, 14 and 15.

United States (only while at war)

  • +5 Allies control France. +5 Allies control the Philippines. +5 Allies control all three US mainland territories. +5 Allies control 3 of Midway, Wake, Hawaii, Solomon Islands.
  • −15 Neutrality Acts: while at peace with all Axis powers.

China: one infantry for every two territories China controls, placed in China (max 3 per territory). Chinese units may not leave China.

Victory

Projection of Power is on by default. The Axis win by holding 13 victory cities at the end of a round. The Allies begin with 13 (counting the neutral USA and USSR), so they must hold 17 – in practice, taking Berlin, Rome and Tokyo.

Rules

World War II v3 rules, technology and units, as in the 1941 and 1942 scenarios. Neutral countries may be attacked; aircraft may not fly over them.
