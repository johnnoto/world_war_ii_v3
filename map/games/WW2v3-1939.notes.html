World War II v3 – September 1939

1 September 1939. The Wehrmacht crosses into Poland. Two days later Britain and France declare war. The Soviet Union has just signed a non-aggression pact with Germany, Italy is a "non-belligerent" bound to Berlin by the Pact of Steel, Japan has been at war in China for two years, and the United States is officially neutral. Nobody yet knows how wide this war will become – that is up to you.

France is a full playable power in this scenario, with its own army, fleet and colonial empire. One round is roughly six months: round 1 is autumn 1939, round 2 spring–summer 1940, round 4 summer 1941, round 5 the winter of 1941–42.

At the start of its first turn, each power receives a briefing: its historical situation in September 1939 and the rules that matter most to it.

Neutral Countries

Poland, East Poland, the Low Countries, Norway, the Baltic States, Yugoslavia and Greece (Balkans), Persia and Iceland begin neutral. Most have armies of their own (orange units) and can be invaded by anyone at no PU cost – they are at war with whoever attacks them. Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey and the other impassable neutrals stay out of the war.

Finland, Romania and Bulgaria, the Dutch East Indies (Borneo, East Indies) and the Belgian Congo are sealed until history opens them (see Events).

Diplomacy

Powers that are at peace with each other cannot attack each other or enter each other's territory. War is declared in the Politics phase, and only when history allows it:

  • Operation Barbarossa – Germany may attack the USSR from round 3.
  • The Soviets strike first – if Germany has not attacked by then, the USSR declares war on Germany automatically at the end of its round-5 turn.
  • Japan may declare war on France (Occupy Indochina) from round 1. From round 2 it may also declare war on Britain, the USA, Britain and the USA together (Strike South), or the USSR (Strike North), each separately and only when it chooses.
  • Germany declares war on the USA from round 5.
  • The United States enters the war automatically at the end of its round-4 turn – round 3 after a crisis in Indo-China (see Events) – against Germany, Italy and Japan. Before that it may declare war on Germany at once if the Axis take the United Kingdom (and is forced to, see Events); on Japan once Japan declares war on Britain – but not if Britain declares war on Japan; and on Italy once at war with Germany.
  • Britain declares war on Japan – possible once Japan has attacked France (Occupy Indochina) while France was still fighting Germany.
  • August Storm – the USSR may declare war on Japan from round 4, or once Germany has fallen.

Alliances form automatically between powers fighting a common enemy: Germany and Italy once Italy enters the war, Britain and the USSR after Barbarossa, the Grand Alliance after Pearl Harbor, and so on. Vichy France joins no one.

Events

  • Italy enters the war automatically on its round-2 turn, against Britain (and France if France is still fighting), and becomes Germany's ally. Italy sits out round 1 only.
  • German sphere. If Italy captures France, Northwestern Europe, Poland, Bulgaria-Romania, Norway or Finland, the territory goes to Germany instead (Italian units there stay Italian).
  • French factory destroyed. The first time France passes to Germany – taken by Germany, or by Italy and handed over – the French factory is removed from the game at the end of that combat phase.
  • Fall of France. If the Axis still hold France when Germany's next turn begins – so Britain and France have had a full round to retake Paris – the Armistice of Compiègne is signed during Germany's turn:
    • Morocco-Algeria and its garrison go over to Germany.
    • French West Africa, French Equatorial Africa and Madagascar, with their garrisons, go over to Britain.
    • French Indo-China stays with Vichy France but loses its garrison. Japan may occupy it (Occupy Indochina) with no wider consequences.
    • The French navy is removed from the game. If France still has any warship, Britain receives one cruiser at Scapa Flow (sea zone 3) – or off Halifax (sea zone 9) if the Axis hold the United Kingdom.
    • All other French units are disbanded and France leaves the war for good. Paris can no longer be liberated: from then on, whoever takes France keeps it.
  • Germany reinforced. If Germany attacks France, and on France's next turn the French still hold Paris and build new units in France, Germany receives 5 infantry in Germany at the end of France's turn (once per game). If France cannot build there – for example its factory has been bombed out – Germany gets nothing.
  • India falls. If Japan takes India, the United States declares war on Japan at the end of that Japanese combat phase.
  • The southern advance. If Japan holds Borneo, the East Indies and French Indo-China at the same time, the United States declares war on Japan at the end of that Japanese combat phase.
  • Crisis in Indo-China. If Japan declares war on France while France is still fighting Germany, Britain may declare war on Japan, the USA enters the war a round early (at the end of its round-3 turn), and the USA places 1 infantry and 1 AA gun each in Hawaii and the Philippines.
  • Supply routes into China. At the start of each Chinese Place Units step, China receives 1 artillery in Yunnan if France holds French Indo-China (the Hanoi–Kunming railway), and 1 more if Britain holds Burma (the Burma Road), as long as China holds Yunnan.
  • Belgian Congo. When the Axis take the Low Countries, the Belgian Congo joins Britain – or France, if the Axis already hold the United Kingdom while France is still fighting and holds Paris.
  • Dutch East Indies. Borneo and the East Indies stay neutral and closed until Japan is at war with Britain. In the Politics phase where that war begins, they join Britain with 1 infantry each, so Japan can attack them that same turn.
  • Romania and Bulgaria join the Axis at the start of Germany's round 3 combat move, with their armies (2 infantry, 1 artillery).
  • Finland joins Germany at the end of Germany's round 4 turn – too late for Finland to attack that turn – with its army (3 infantry, 1 artillery). Until then Finland is neutral and closed to everyone.
  • Britain falls. If the Axis take the United Kingdom, the United States and the Soviet Union immediately declare war on Germany, at the end of that combat phase, and join France and the British Empire. Japan may then declare war on what remains of the British Empire at once, even in round 1.
  • The government moves to Ottawa. When London falls, the King and Parliament cross to Canada: Eastern Canada becomes a second British capital and gets a factory, so Britain keeps collecting income, building and fighting with the rest of the Empire. The PUs Britain held when London fell still go to whoever took it. (This needs Britain to hold Eastern Canada; otherwise it happens as soon as Britain does.)
  • Italy automatically declares war on the USSR when Germany is at war with it. Germany and Italy declare war on the United States once Japan is at war with Britain and the USA.

China: one infantry for every two territories China controls, placed in China (max 3 per territory). Chinese units may not leave China.

Victory

Projection of Power is on by default. The Axis win by holding 13 victory cities at the end of a round. The Allies begin with 13 (counting the neutral USA and USSR), so they must hold 17 – in practice, taking Berlin, Rome and Tokyo.

Rules

World War II v3 rules, technology and units, as in the 1941 and 1942 scenarios. Neutral countries may be attacked; aircraft may not fly over them.
